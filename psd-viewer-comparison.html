Which one should you pick?

  • Pick Photopea when the job needs deep Photoshop-style editing: complex layer styles, smart objects, advanced filters, or very large Photoshop files.
  • Pick freePS when you want the quickest private path: open a PSD, see every layer, make a light edit, export PNG/JPG/WEBP — with the file parsed locally and never uploaded.
  • Pick Figpea or Photoemu when you want another free browser option for viewing and editing, and you are happy to compare their interfaces yourself.
  • Pick GIMP or Krita when you work offline, need a full desktop editor, or your PSDs are part of a larger illustration workflow.

Why “no upload” matters

Most PSD jobs involve someone else's work: a client file, an unreleased design, a template under NDA. A viewer that parses the file in your browser keeps that file on your device. A viewer that uploads it to a server puts it somewhere you do not control. For everyday personal files it makes no difference; for client work it is the deciding factor.

A note on accuracy

Free tools change their free tiers, ads, and limits without notice. The table above reflects each tool's public pages in October 2026. If a detail decides your choice — especially whether a tool uploads your file — re-check that tool's own privacy page before opening client work in it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free PSD viewer?

It depends on the job. Photopea is the most full-featured free browser editor. freePS is the lightest private option for viewing and light edits. GIMP and Krita are the best free desktop editors.

Do free PSD viewers upload my file?

Photopea and freePS parse files locally in your browser, so the file never leaves your device. Always check a tool's privacy page before opening client or NDA work in it.

Can I edit a PSD without Photoshop?

Yes. Photopea offers the deepest browser editing. freePS covers light edits — layers, text, brushes, filters — and exports PNG, JPG, WEBP, or a JSON project file.

Is freePS really free?

Yes. freePS is free for personal and commercial use, with no account, no subscription and no ads.

When should I use a desktop app instead?

When you work offline, need full desktop editing power, or your PSDs feed into a larger illustration workflow — GIMP and Krita are free, open-source desktop editors.

What if I only need to view a PSD, not edit it?

Use the freePS PSD viewer: drop the file, see every layer, export an image if you need one. No account, no upload, no install.
