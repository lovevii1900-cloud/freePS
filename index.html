Quick Start: How to Edit in 4 Simple Steps

Get started editing your graphics without any learning curve.

01

Open or Drop Image

Click 'Launch Editor' and use 'File -> Open' or simply paste an image from your clipboard using Ctrl+V.

02

Adjust & Retouch

Use the left toolbar to crop dimensions, brush details, add text annotations, or apply filters from the top menu.

03

Manage Layers

Control different elements through the right-hand Layers panel. Duplicate, reorder, or tweak layer transparency.

04

Export in High Quality

Save your finished work locally to PNG, JPG, WEBP, or JSON layer project file via 'File -> Save'.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FreePS completely free to use?

Yes, FreePS is 100% free for both personal and commercial use without account registration or subscription fees.

Are my private photos uploaded to any servers?

No. FreePS is built purely on client-side web technologies. Every drawing operation and picture modification is executed within your local computer's browser memory.

What image formats can I export?

You can export your artwork to PNG (transparent), JPG, WEBP, and raw JSON project files for later re-editing.

Can I open and view Adobe Photoshop (.PSD) files online?

Yes, FreePS allows you to preview and edit layer graphics directly in your browser without requiring an Adobe Creative Cloud subscription.
