Frequently Asked Questions

How to open a PSD file without Photoshop online?

Simply drag and drop your .psd file into the viewer box at the top of psdrawing.com. freePS parses the Photoshop binary format directly in your browser and lets you preview or export it to PNG/JPG immediately.

Is freePS completely free to use?

Yes, freePS is 100% free for both personal and commercial use without account registration or subscription fees.

Are my private PSD files or photos uploaded to any servers?

No. freePS is built purely on client-side web technologies. Every PSD layer extraction and picture modification is executed within your local computer's browser memory.

What image formats can I export?

You can export your artwork to PNG (transparent), JPG, WEBP, and raw JSON project files for later re-editing.

Can I use freePS as a free Photoshop alternative?

Yes, freePS allows you to preview PSD files and edit multi-layer graphics directly in your browser without requiring an expensive Adobe Creative Cloud subscription.
