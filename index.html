About FreePS - Free Online Photo Editor

FreePS is an in-browser graphics editor designed for editing photos, creating digital artwork, and sketching without installing heavy software. It runs entirely on client-side web technologies, providing quick and responsive access to layers, brush adjustments, filters, and standard image manipulation tools.

Features

  • Layer Manipulation: Create, hide, blend, and organize multiple layers.
  • Creative Drawing Tools: Adjustable brushes, erasers, text tools, and color pickers.
  • No Installation Needed: Works directly in any modern browser on desktop and mobile.
