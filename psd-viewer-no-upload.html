How no-upload PSD viewing works

  1. The web app loads. Your browser downloads the freePS page and editing code, just like any website.
  2. You choose a local file. The PSD comes from your computer, phone or connected storage.
  3. JavaScript parses it locally. Layers are decoded and drawn in browser memory on your device.
  4. Exports download locally. PNG, JPG or WEBP files are generated on your device and saved by your browser.

The important distinction is not whether a website is involved — every web tool is — but whether your PSD has to be transferred to and stored by that website. With local processing, it does not.

How to check the privacy claim yourself

You do not have to take any tool's word for it. In a desktop browser, open the developer tools Network panel before adding a PSD and watch whether the file is sent in an upload request. A local tool will load its app files from the web, but your PSD should not be posted to a server. Frequently asked questions

Are my PSD files stored by freePS?
No. The PSD is parsed locally in your browser; freePS does not need to receive or store it.

Can I export PNG after viewing privately?
Yes. Use File → Save in the editor to export PNG, JPG or WEBP. The PSD to PNG guide shows the layer workflow.

Is “online” automatically unsafe?
No. The question is how the tool handles the file. A client-side web app can be more private than an upload service or an unknown desktop download.

What are the limits?
Very large files depend on your device memory, and complex Photoshop features may render differently. For regulated or high-risk material, follow your employer's approved tools policy.
