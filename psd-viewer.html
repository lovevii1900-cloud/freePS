When to Use PNG vs. JPG vs. WEBP When Converting PSDs

PNG (Portable Network Graphics): Best for UI mockups, logos, icons, and PSD files containing transparent backgrounds or sharp typography where lossless pixel accuracy is required.

JPG (Joint Photographic Experts Group): Ideal for photographic compositions, digital paintings, and print-ready posters where compact file size is preferred and transparency is not needed.

WEBP: Recommended for modern websites and e-commerce product graphics, delivering both alpha transparency and 30%+ smaller file sizes compared to PNG.
