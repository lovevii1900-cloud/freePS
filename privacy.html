Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

At freePS (accessible from https://psdrawing.com/), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us.

1. Client-Side Image Processing & Storage

freePS is a client-side web application. All PSD files, drawings, photos, and layers are parsed and processed locally within your browser memory and are never transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard websites, we may collect anonymous usage statistics to monitor performance and improve browser compatibility.

3. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@psdrawing.com
