Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At FreePS (accessible from psdrawing.com), the privacy of our visitors is of extreme importance to us.

1. Image Processing & Storage

FreePS is primarily a client-side web application. All drawings, photos, and files are processed locally within your browser and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Log Files & Analytics

Like many standard websites, we may collect anonymous usage statistics to help us monitor performance and improve our tool.

3. Cookies and Advertising

Third-party vendors, including advertising networks, may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.

4. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@psdrawing.com
